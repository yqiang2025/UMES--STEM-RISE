Program Overview

Build research experience for graduate, medical, or professional school.

STEM-RISE gives UMES undergraduate students hands-on research experience in STEM-related fields. Students work with an approved mentor, participate in career-development activities, and strengthen their preparation for competitive graduate school applications.

Students may identify a research mentor whose work aligns with their academic and career goals. Mentors may include faculty members, researchers, or professionals, subject to program approval.

Eligibility

Who should apply

  • Current UMES undergraduate student in a STEM-related discipline.
  • Interest in research and STEM-related career pathways.
  • Strong academic potential and commitment to continued study.
  • Interest in graduate, medical, or professional school preparation.
